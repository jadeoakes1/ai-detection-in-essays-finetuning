Once upon a time, in a meadow nestled between two ancient oak trees, there lived a young fairy named Elara who had only just grown her wings three moons ago. Unlike the other fairies in her village, Elara's wings were a deep violet color, shimmering with flecks of silver that caught the moonlight in the most spectacular way. She was curious and adventurous, always wandering too far from home despite her mother's warnings about the world beyond the meadow. One crisp autumn morning, Elara discovered a tiny injured sparrow lying beneath a pile of fallen leaves, its wing bent at an odd angle. Though she had never attempted healing magic before, Elara closed her eyes and pressed her small glowing hands gently against the bird's feathers, humming the ancient melody she had once heard the elder fairies sing during the harvest festival. To her astonishment, a warm golden light poured from her fingertips, and within moments the sparrow ruffled its feathers, chirped softly, and hopped to its feet. The sparrow tilted its head, studying Elara with bright black eyes, then launched itself gracefully into the sky. Elara watched it soar above the treetops, her heart swelling with a joy she had never felt before, and she finally understood what her true gift was meant to be.
