Making scrambled eggs is one of the simplest and most satisfying cooking tasks you can master. Start by cracking two or three eggs into a bowl, then add a small splash of milk or cream along with a pinch of salt and pepper. Whisk everything together until the yolks and whites are fully combined and the mixture looks uniform and slightly frothy. Heat a non-stick pan over medium-low heat and add a small pat of butter, letting it melt slowly without browning. Pour in the egg mixture and let it sit undisturbed for just a moment before gently pushing it around the pan with a spatula. The key is to move the eggs slowly and fold them rather than stirring aggressively, which helps create soft, creamy curds instead of dry, rubbery pieces. Remove the pan from the heat while the eggs still look slightly underdone, as they will continue cooking from the residual heat. Serve them immediately on a warm plate, and feel free to top them with fresh herbs, shredded cheese, or a dash of hot sauce for extra flavor.
